## What changed in the evaluator

- Name the source-answerable discoveries before layout. Show at least three actual reading routes, covering two operations and two body regions. A question printed in a sidebar is not a graphical answer.
- Bind images to exact visible leaves, releases or dated records. For example, the printed category code and an elbow connector must identify the pictured instrument. Metadata and approximate proximity do not prove ownership.
- Preserve a continuous explanatory network. Paired lookup codes can supplement it, but cannot replace the principal paths being evaluated.
- Reserve the complete text, date, image and caption footprint before packing. Check routed connections against all text and image obstacles, including their own source and target records.
- Retain separate illustrated, exploratory and reference-target outcomes. A high score on one dimension cannot erase an unresolved composition defect. Reference acceptance also requires an independently verified semantic density minimum.
- Compare two fully illustrated candidates using whole and detail images and a relevant reference, without supplying the author's diagnosis. Retain disagreement.
- Bind each comparison verdict to a visible neutral identifier, exact image paths, the chart's own title and a distinctive layout fingerprint. Reject a label/prose mismatch instead of silently changing the vote.
