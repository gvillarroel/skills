## Development attempts

Twelve explorations render complete graphs and twelve fail or exhaust their bounded search. A complete geometric pass is not automatic visual acceptance.

- Vertical-only chronology/stage experiments require more height. They are not accepted by deleting institutions or shrinking type.
- The two-coordinate full-size experiments produce 90 and 77 crossings. Several smaller CP searches return `UNKNOWN` within 45–60 seconds; that means no accepted solution within the budget, not proven infeasibility.
- Greedy attempts that overweight horizontal neighborhoods fail on late descendants. Allowing more lateral movement gives a complete compact seed, but 125 crossings make it unsuitable as the final result.
- Outward-rounded integral envelopes remove the fractional-seed collisions and attachment violations. A complete integral seed has 121 crossings; CP refinement reduces this to 86. Increasing the crossing-proxy penalty then produces 89 actual crossings, and quadratic smoothing leaves 86 while adding bends. Neither is a better final candidate.
- Lateral influence prototypes reduce the compact candidate to 77 crossings and the old full-size candidate to 58. The final selection favors the compact candidate's legibility and earlier optical expansion, while explicitly retaining the crossing tradeoff.

Early attempts used an evolving driver without per-attempt snapshots. Later attempts preserve their exact driver bytes and hashes. Retain the outcomes and their actual evidence; do not claim all early attempts are independently frozen reproductions.
