## What changed and why

The prior composer measured the institution panel but did not reserve a wide family caption attached to it. Replaying the exact five-caption request against the retained 104-file v32 bundle fails at `THE CIVIC PRESS`. That frozen bundle, digest `85f1eb0bf430e1b20692642d419d0c508ec75fba72bbcf0f8a3b7624997cdba4`, remains unchanged. The completed control without those five captions preserves all 70 institutions and 97 relationships.

The new composer packs each complete institution-plus-caption envelope, including wrapped heading text and artwork. It preserves the actual name-panel width, supplied font sizes and explicit offsets. Resolved captions remain protected when the source is rendered again. The browser audit checks the actual visible label, paint, position, type size, artwork and unrelated connection paths. A family pill may label its own incoming trunk.

The initial agent tests exposed a separate source-validation trap: layout resolution adds coordinates, ports, route bends and caption offsets. Whole-array equality therefore reports changes even when every original field is intact. A small supplied-field verifier now matches stable identities and allows additional layout fields while requiring all original records and values.

The next cohort exposed a more consequential delivery failure: two agents supplied a different JSON from the one actually rendered. The coupled `create_branch_poster.py` command now writes the composed source and its SVG/HTML together, checks original fields, and audits those exact final paths. It supplies missing editorial/lineage/branches defaults while rejecting explicit incompatible profiles. This operational simplification, without any further change to the naturalistic prompt, passes the final three Luna runs.

The reusable workflow is documented in [data-first-branches.md](../../skills/usefulcharts-style/references/data-first-branches.md). Normal use needs one command:

```sh
uv run --script skills/usefulcharts-style/scripts/create_branch_poster.py draft.json --output-dir result
```

## Visual critique and rejected alternatives

Compared with the [UsefulCharts Christian Denominations Family Tree](https://usefulcharts.com/products/christian-denominations-family-tree), the medium poster now has clearer local family titles. The restrained background, semantic category color, compact names, emphasized institutions and source-bound connections provide a useful foundation. The reference and fictional development case contain different data; the side-by-side display is qualitative evidence only.

The largest remaining differences are visible at both page and detail scale:

1. Broad families persist for most of the page. The five new titles improve navigation but do not by themselves create the richer sequence of local stories in the reference.
2. Dotted influences span large distances and run alongside structural branches. Their technical clearance is sound, but tracing them still takes effort, especially around the left illustration cluster and late digital institutions.
3. Ordinary records repeat a similar name-and-note treatment. The relatively few emphasized panels and illustrations do not yet create enough local variation in hierarchy and visual identity.

Two complete 141-institution, 171-relation reflows test whether automatic placement can improve the current institutional mural. The initial founded-order version fails to place a caption, and the initial causal-order version exceeds a routing budget. After the envelope correction, both main graphs render, but the founded version has 112 crossings and a 39.18-unit unrelated shared run; the causal version has 114 crossings and a 129.4-unit shared run. The current published mural has 76 crossings and no such warning. Crossing counts are diagnostics, not aesthetic scores.

Both complete reflows are visually weaker: long vertical stretches in one, a wide fan and long cross-family routes in the other. Their original contextual insets are retained separately and omitted from the probes, so they are not complete replacement deliverables in any event. Two nodes-only diagnostics also remain explicitly labeled as diagnostics. None replaces the gallery mural.

The genealogy and parallel-history examples remain unchanged. This revision does not resolve their repeated nameplate rhythm or regular regional treatment. Overall quality and composition equivalence remain open.
