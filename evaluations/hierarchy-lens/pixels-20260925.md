## Visual review and limits

Inspected the 1200-record overview, role and token lenses, focused pixels, image-only mode, mobile layout, 430-person outputs, and the eight-record portfolio. The image changes from concentrated role sectors to local numeric variations while its regions remain fixed. The portfolio has visibly coarse square steps; larger organizations require finer grids and zoom for small regions.

Pixels compose a record's region; a pixel is not a person. Angle represents subtree headcount, but pixel area cannot compare headcount across rings. The builder refines grids up to 2048 to avoid dropping records and rejects topologies that still cannot fit; it does not claim arbitrary-scale completeness. The numeric default uses global quantiles of positive observations, with linear and log1p alternatives. Missing observations use a checkerboard and zero uses the darkest band. Static PNGs contain no visible legend; their context remains in the HTML and SVG metadata.
