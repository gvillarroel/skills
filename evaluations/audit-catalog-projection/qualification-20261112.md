## Interpretation limits

Field preservation under an explicit scope does not establish the correctness
of a source ontology, natural-language equivalence, OWL consistency or faithful
free-text extraction. A same-label match does not establish identity. Equal
fingerprints are scope-bound structural equality, and are not an RFC8785/JCS
interoperability guarantee. Unselected fields remain outside the claim.

Use the general ontology-authoring and ontology-guided extraction workflows for
creating or applying vocabularies. This capability can check whether a later
JSON export discarded their declared fields. Neither a successful helper nor a
new skill can rescue a different skill's failed promotion gate.
