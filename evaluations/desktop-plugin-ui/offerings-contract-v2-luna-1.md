in the recommendation to have its scoped historical record. This is a factual

The copied Copilot reference attributes `canvases:true` to the **terminal
session's negotiated UI capabilities**. Its separate ACP initialization

unsupported ACP surface assignment. Keep the actual terminal/session scope
or omit the unsupported attribution. This does not mean ACP can never expose
such data, and the artifact correctly leaves canvas paint untested; the
failure is the identity of the historical measurement claimed here.
