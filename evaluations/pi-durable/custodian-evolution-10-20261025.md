workspace, not an operating-system confidentiality boundary. Manual source and
compatibility review is an additional activation requirement. Do not tune a
candidate from reserved feedback within this study.

## Practical limits

The study measures bounded synthetic decision and receipt behavior, with actual
disposable worker effects. It does not prove general maintenance quality, all
rename/copy/path forms, every validation timeout/unavailable case, future prompt
adherence, native macOS execution or successful future unattended GitHub work.
Production quality needs task-attributed outcomes and independent verification.
Retaining a compatible baseline after an unsafe search result is a valid release
decision; ten attempts do not promise ten improvements or ten deployments.
