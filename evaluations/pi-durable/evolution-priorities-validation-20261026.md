## Why these priorities follow the evidence

The completed study's selected pair improved the composite entirely through
worker receipt coverage, with zero measured decision-score gain. Its single
batch per arm does not establish independent repetition or decision-quality
confidence. Zero baseline receipt utility did not mean configured checks never
ran. Preserve those distinctions before choosing more expensive tests.

All ten related descendants failed a separate existing-consumer capability
contract. That supports an early deterministic regression test, not an estimate
of production failure frequency from ten independent samples. The final release
review also found an oversized serialized response and lost rename identity.
A local effect followed by failure lacked newly promised state identities;
this is incomplete new provenance, rather than automatic removal of an old
field. These are development/release observations; no reserved cases were read.

Useful controls already demonstrated their scope: the unsafe worker negative
control failed; valid native faux mechanics passed while a null improvement was
rejected; retained Windows path failures led to a fixture-only preflight repair;
immutable source/selection seals preserved lineage; and compatibility rejection
prevented activation and reserved-gate release. Successful hard flags still did
not establish whole-task quality or cover every consumer.

The resulting order is conditional on risk, objective and local cost:

1. Check attribution, environment and evaluator controls without inference.
   For each patch, reject current permission/API/consumer and real host-envelope
   failures before paying for quality batches.
2. Reconcile actual worker attempts and effects with public and persisted
   evidence, including no-op and partial failure. Retain known working guards
   and prove the evaluator rejects false observations, not merely empty fields.
3. On eligible source, use predeclared paired sampling and component constraints
   for task decisions and useful outcomes. Keep receipt completeness separate;
   one batch or higher scalar cannot prove better model judgment.
4. Freeze one eligible finalist before one-way independent gates and separately
   authorized activation/duty checks. Native platform requirements follow the
   intended claim: macOS is required for public two-platform support, while a
   clearly Windows-only evaluation may proceed with that limitation recorded.

These are operator choices to implement in the next trusted verifier/precheck,
not universal weights, budget ratios or a mandatory stage count. Development
diagnostics may guide declared execution priority and the next hypothesis;
fixtures, graders, component thresholds, selection and budgets stay frozen
within a study. New assertions belong to a new protocol, without retroactively
rewriting old scores. Reserved feedback driving a change ends that study.
