  at a nonbinary scale, 1280 × 720.
- Boundary: one isolated Luna run rejecting insufficient tank capacity without
  changing source facts or writing misleading composition outputs.
- Naturalistic inlet: three fresh workspaces using the unchanged
  [eight-second inlet prompt](../pi-prompts/hyperframes-explainer-assets.md).
- Generalization vehicle: three fresh workspaces using the unchanged
  [colorset2 vehicle prompt](../pi-prompts/hyperframes-explainer-vehicle-assets.md).
- Sol-quality video: three fresh workspaces using the
  [Full HD two-event prompt](../pi-prompts/hyperframes-explainer-sol-quality.md).

Each video case needs at least two joint passes out of three on the same frozen
runtime payload. Joint success requires strict isolation/model/exact-output/
zero-tool-error/integrity checks, the independent numerical/browser/media
evaluator and direct visual review. Retain every attempt. No prior fixture or
Sol artifact is exposed to the tested agent.

The Full HD case preserves 16 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, colorset1, an 80 L
tank, a 2→5→1 L/s input with ramps at t=4..6 and t=10..12, and an exact 45 L
final state. It additionally requires the changing gate, connected transport,
actual graduated ruler and both fixed-scale histories. This case checks the
requested quality at the Sol video's scale, rather than relying only on smaller
smoke output.
