## Shared acceptance rule

Freeze retained facts/relations, full labels, readable delivery-size text,
stroke/head envelopes, requested dimensions/orientation and meaningful motion
before reducing space. Measure content-sized nodes, remove excess wrappers,
compare orientation/order where semantics permit, shorten clear routes and
give unrelated edges separate lanes/ports. A crossing must remain traceable
as a crossing rather than an implied junction.

Compare each tighter local candidate at the same display scale. Reject any
overlap that obscures reading or route attribution, clipped/truncated label,
hidden head, ambiguous endpoint, merged
unrelated connection, contrast loss or insufficient movement space. Restore
the failed local clearance and retain the smallest passing candidate. Stop
when the remaining gaps protect reading/routing or another concrete reduction
fails. Do not shrink fonts, drop facts or claim a global packing optimum.
Quantitative charts retain useful information dimensions, comparable scales,
axes, legends and distinguishable marks.

## Acceptance and limits

Every changed bundle requires a passing deterministic contract and at least
two joint strict/independent passes among three fresh natural repetitions
of a frozen geometry payload. Boundaries are added where the change touches
refusal, routing or complete-label behavior. The component reports retain
the exact hashes and identify narrow later repairs separately; they do not
combine passes from source-changing cohorts into a fictional final cohort.

The helpers support their documented topology and input limits. Longer
labels enlarge only their affected node/row; excessive traffic gets its own
lane/gutter; impossible explicit canvases are refused. Complex graphs may
need related subdiagrams or a specialist native layout. Compaction remains
an evidence-based local search with preserved information, rather than a
proof of the mathematical optimum or certification of every future output.
Browser bounds alone do not establish readability: all accepted visual
artifacts also receive delivery-size inspection of actual painted output.
Traceable native underpasses may retain a short white caption backing over
an aligned shaft when both ends and direction remain clear; this is not a
claim of zero physical intersections. Unrelated merged connections and
obscured arrows remain failures.
