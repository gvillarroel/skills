## Reusable workflow

The primary skill routes real contributor/brand tasks to a compact
`people-and-logos.md` reference and `media_assets.py`. The helper searches a
bounded Commons candidate list, requires reviewed identity and contribution
evidence, retains local originals/digests/rights, reuses matching acquisitions,
and produces circular SVG, transparent PNG, credits and graph-node manifests.
It preserves complete vector logos with centered meet scaling inside a separate
circular support. Nested data-URI SVG is accepted after recursive static-vector
validation; raster or remote logo dependencies are not called vector artwork.
Portrait SVGs explicitly retain real raster photo bytes. Namesake verification,
historical affiliations and source-date distinctions remain narrative decisions.

The adapter documents the actually observed native Replace video → Upload
path. It preserves placement and original narration while requiring checks of
the selected scene, new visual pixels, object count, duration and voice track.
An unchanged filename is insufficient evidence. No helper silently operates UI
or converts retained observations into new account actions.
