# ambientCG format and material selection

Find three free brick materials from ambientCG. Save the choices to
`artifacts/options.json` and `artifacts/options.html`. I choose saved option 2;
download that exact material's 1K PNG ZIP package to `artifacts/brick.zip` and
its receipt to `artifacts/brick.zip.json`. Write `artifacts/review.md` with the
selected ID, package attributes and included map inventory. Do not substitute
a JPG package. The copied `skills/ambientcg-material-search/` is read-only.
