# Kenney pack and member selection

Find three free nature packs in Kenney's 3D category. Save the choices to
`artifacts/options.json` and `artifacts/options.html`. I choose saved option 2;
download that exact pack to `artifacts/nature.zip` with its source receipt at
`artifacts/nature.zip.json`. Inspect the ZIP inventory and extract only its
license notice to a new directory `artifacts/license-only`, including an
extraction receipt at `artifacts/license-only/extraction.json`. Write
`artifacts/review.md` with the selected pack ID and retained license path.
Keep the copied `skills/kenney-asset-search/` bundle read-only.
