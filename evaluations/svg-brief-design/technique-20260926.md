# Purpose-specific SVG technique calibration — 2026-09-26

This is a public development recalibration following the user's inspection of
the eight tied or unresolved comparisons. It preserves all generated SVGs and
purchased originals. It changes the evaluation contract and visual evidence,
not the skill or its measured generation performance. No private cohort was
opened.

## Corrected target

The earlier evaluation gave generic clarity, smoothness and recognizability too
much credit without establishing whether those decisions served the intended
artifact. The user's correction concerns technique, composition and editorial
economy, not matching reference paths or rewarding provenance.

| Family | More discriminating technical evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Vintage diagrams | Related substantial strokes; axes and trace carry the explanation; only indispensable notation; a coherent requested print idiom rather than a modern measured plot with serif text. |
| Space insignia | Black marks and bounded white gaps jointly construct an implied body; continuity, closure and connected axes; deliberate mass distribution instead of an undifferentiated slab. |
| Circular ornaments | Integrated band-to-void transitions, consistent taper and purposeful junctions; distinguish individually smooth curves from a composed group. |
| Compact labels | A small occupied format, few useful identifiers or navigation cues, and readable economy; distinguish a compact identification strip from an equipment specification record. |

These are conditional standards. Neither thicker strokes nor fewer elements
are inherently better. A contemporary measured plot and a specification record
have different information requirements. The
[design contract](../../projects/svg-brief-design/evaluation/technique-design-contract.md)
contains transferable generation operations and short natural-language briefs,
without source geometry or exact counts. The selected print idiom is a project
art direction, not a universal historical claim about old books.

## Method

Jev is the final judge, requested `typesafe/jev-1.13` and retaining the observed
dated model identifier in every native report. A tool-free GPT-6 Astra observer
views the paired renders and thumbnails. Neither model receives source status,
purchase identity, previous scores, candidate binding or an expected winner.

The revised visual prompt requires located relationships before adjectives:
where a band joins, what bounds an opening, which crossings trap narrow slivers,
how added fields affect the label's footprint, and how stroke character serves
the requested medium. It cannot infer actual physical size, hidden path quality
or mechanical reproduction from the normalized raster views.

Six artistic dimensions retain their existing weights: silhouette 20%, spatial
composition 20%, rhythm 15%, craft 20%, information hierarchy 10%, style 15%.
Each uses five concrete ordered levels in native Jev `Score`. TypeSafe documents
[Score for ordered spectra](https://docs.typesafe.ai/primitives/score); unordered
overall preference, subject fulfillment and evidence sufficiency remain
categorical decisions. The typed response validator is unchanged.

The numerical reward is an **expected coded utility** using category points
50, 75, 90, 95 and 100, oriented relative to the candidate. Provider probability
rounding is normalized before aggregation. Pure parity is 90 by convention;
this does not mean that every original was independently assigned an absolute
90/100 artistic grade. A flat probability distribution has expected utility
82 but a signed directional margin of zero. Therefore the report also publishes
that margin and global preference; a lower mean utility alone cannot establish
correct artistic discrimination. Confidence does not multiply quality.

Unknown subject or insufficient evidence produces no numeric reward. Wrong
subject is zero; major missing content caps the reward at 49 and a supported
secondary mismatch at 79. Existing SVG validity checks remain separate. This
scale is not numerically interchangeable with v5.2's conservative category
bounds, and its decimals are not measured aesthetic precision.

from that scalar. This limitation is retained explicitly rather than normalizing
