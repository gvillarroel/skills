matched development comparisons. No improvement or promotion is claimed.

The user requested continuing improvement until 2026-09-26 06:00
America/New_York (10:00 UTC). Work began at 09:33 UTC. The completed experiment
retained the exact GPT-6 Luna model, medium reasoning, SSE transport, native
Harbor 0.18.0/GEPA 0.1.2 runtime, unchanged verifier, and private gate rules.

The [native quality adapter](../../projects/svg-brief-design/scripts/pi_svg_skill_sse.py)
loads `SKILL.md` through Pi's explicit skill command and exposes only `write`.
Supporting reference files are present but the model has no tool to open them,
browse their links, or render its own output in that condition. These results
therefore evaluate the loaded instructions under that restricted access.
The independent routing controls use `read` or `read,write`. A future study of
reference consultation or rendered self-correction must give both arms matched
access and register the tool change as a separate factor.

Independent development-only review found that the prior nine proposals mostly
rewrote broad design advice. Some apparent reference mismatches are legitimate
choices left open by the short briefs. The new hypothesis is a minimal additive
prompt-binding checkpoint: associate each explicit modifier with an object,
part, or relationship, then verify that the dominant forms express those
requirements before writing the SVG. The validator requires every original
word to remain in order, with only 30–180 added words. Metadata and resources
stay unchanged; artwork and reconstruction recipes remain prohibited.

Six tasks are selected by a prospective hash rule, one per development family.
Only two families have unused public cases, so this cohort contains two newly
tested tasks and four declared prior-exposure tasks. Both arms are freshly
measured. No previous outcomes are replaced, pooled, or rerun as recovery.

Budget: one proposal, at most 18 development metric calls and 30 private gate
trials, three workers, no retries. Validation and holdout retain three attempts
per task and side and each require +0.015 mean gain. No new trial calls are
admitted after 09:55 UTC. An incomplete or unqualified result cannot be promoted.
All started evidence remains preserved. The unchanged original remains installed.

six initial original-guide observations, three fresh original-guide minibatch
observations, and three observations of the one additive proposal. All 12 passed
the runtime, exact-model, input-integrity, provenance and artifact checks. There
were no execution failures, non-evaluable trials, provider retries or task
replacements. GEPA's selected pool contains only the original guide: its
