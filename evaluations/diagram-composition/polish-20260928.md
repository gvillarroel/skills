## Scope and changes

The user identified visible lines continuing inside boxes and inconsistent color
application. This follow-up keeps the original concept-first decomposition,
visual-family selection, weighted grid spans, shared SVG identities, and exact
display-size contract. It improves rendered object/connector geometry, complete
semantic surfaces, readable contrast, and compact native panel placement.

- Ports now bind to real exported objects. The composer derives their positions
  and outward directions, routes around leaf nodes, favors shared gutters, and
  separates parallel routes. Containers remain traversable when a connection
  targets a child. Invalid routes produce actionable diagnostics.
- Native panels export object/port bindings and internal wire owners. Imported
  rects/circles/ellipses can be measured in the browser, including nested scaling
  and offset viewBoxes. Source-internal wires are checked against actual rounded
  and elliptical surfaces, including their visible ends after opaque occlusion.
  An intentionally open external terminal requires a named relationship.
- Browser checks catch visible node intrusion, floating/wrong-side contacts,
  crossings, coincident wires, obscured accents, text interference, conflicting
  fills beside correct borders, and insufficient text contrast on solid surfaces.
  Official logo artwork retains its own colors; semantic enclosures carry the
  shared concept palette.
- Boundary cards and taxonomy contents use their real content budgets instead of
  stretching sparse text through tall cells. Draft compose inspection saves
  diagnostics; final compose/audit still fail on unresolved findings.
- A complete native pipeline now builds, measures, routes, composes, audits, and
  previews from one authored brief. It preserves supplied inputs and regenerates
  owned outputs repeatably. Elliptical hub nodes use inscribed text budgets, and
  hubs may sit inside a named enclosure. Measured text/internal-wire obstacles
  and reserved panel headings prevent cross-view routes from cutting through
  source content. A visibility-grid regression covers crossings at grid vertices.

## Limits

The checks require truthful node/wire/color annotations. They measure ordinary
solid surfaces and sample geometry/painter order; arbitrary masks, filters,
complex gradients, unannotated art, and semantic intent still need screenshot
review. Gradient/complex text paint is reported as unverified. No complete
accessibility certification or universal generation success is claimed.

These are public development/forward trials with repeated prompts, not a sealed
benchmark. Strict execution, independent artifacts, and manual visual judgments
are reported separately. No published Pages example or network deployment was
added by this polish.

Accepted trials may retain minor design limitations. In two D naturalistic
trials, exact Photos/Audio/Video labels preserve meaning but the selected generic
pictograms are poor metaphors; the label-only repetition is clearer. Some fixed
grid outputs leave spare lower-right space or use longer perimeter routes.
Acceptance does not certify optimal density or ideal icon selection. These notes
remain in the manual ledger; the delivered Copilot/Claude/cloud example uses
verified, recognizable SVG identities instead of those weak fallback symbols.
