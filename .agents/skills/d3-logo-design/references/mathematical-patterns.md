# Mathematical Logo Patterns

Use this reference when a brief calls for a mathematically constructed mark rather than a decorative geometric variant. Keep every renderer deterministic, use only registered colorset1/colorset2 tokens, and place the complete brand and tagline in the protected lockup region. These patterns declare no intentional text occlusion or omission.

## Construction catalog

| `d3-logo-lorenz-attractor` | `nonlinear-ode-chaotic-trajectory` | Integrate the Lorenz system with fixed-step RK4, discard the transient, project two axes, and simplify only after sampling. | Use the nonlinear ODE trajectory, not a hand-drawn butterfly or closed harmonic curve. |
| `d3-logo-affine-ifs` | `contractive-affine-iteration-invariant` | Iterate a seeded weighted set of contractive affine maps, discard warm-up points, and render the invariant measure. | The mark must be the IFS attractor; avoid L-system branches or random stipple alone. |

## Runtime and validation rules

- Derive all geometry from named parameters and the integer seed. Replaying one configuration must reproduce identical SVG path data.
- Reject non-finite coordinates, singular denominators, unbounded integration, and solver states that leave the protected mark region.
- Use flat registered palette tokens only. Quantize scalar or depth fields to palette roles; do not introduce gradients or computed colors.
- Render the mathematical mark before the generic brand lockup. Keep the full brand and tagline present and unobscured at every normal gallery size.
- Expose the canonical `data-pattern-id` (for example, `d3-logo-lorenz-attractor`), the matching local `data-example-id`, a unique title and description, and the canonical geometry signature.
- Verify the defining invariant, not only the silhouette: constant width, incidence, orthogonality, reciprocal radius, coprimality, 120-degree junctions, or deterministic recurrence as applicable.
- Test 480 px, 240 px, and 96 px widths. Reduce sampling density or texture strength before sacrificing the mathematical invariant or text clearance.
