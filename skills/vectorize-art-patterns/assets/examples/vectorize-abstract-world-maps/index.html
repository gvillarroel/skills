Visual lineage

Both studies borrow the flat color hierarchy and displaced mass rhythm of Biomorphic Drift 03. No path geometry was copied from that pattern.

Geographic provenance

The precise study uses Natural Earth land polygons. The abstract study uses the same public-domain dataset only as a loose placement and relative-scale reference.

Rights
