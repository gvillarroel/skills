How to read this map

Each block is one record. Its angle includes that record and all descendants. Empty outer space means no further reports. Compare angles within a ring, not areas between rings.

Select a legend category to highlight it. Gray hatching means no observation; zero is a measured value. Color scales stay global as you explore.

Keyboard: Tab into the map; arrows inspect blocks, Enter opens a branch, Escape moves up. Search reaches records outside the visible rings.
