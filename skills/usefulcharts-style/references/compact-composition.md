# Compact connected composition

Use this reference for trees, family groups, institutional lineage and multi-concept explanatory compositions. Default to the smallest readable arrangement the actual content can support. Complete information, readable type, visible arrowheads and unambiguous source-to-target routes are hard constraints. Optimize canvas area and avoidable route length only among passing compositions.

Preserve every required record, date, typed relationship, source category, image ownership and explicit geometry. Do not manufacture density, delete required facts, abbreviate literal names, shrink type, clip art or scale the entire poster down to claim compaction. A numeric timeline keeps every measured calendar coordinate and proportional gap; reclaim only local tracks, unused pockets and surrounding margins. Quantitative chart scales and mark dimensions follow the information contract, not diagram packing.

## Reclaim space in the right order

1. Start with the measured data-first route in `SKILL.md`, omitting arbitrary page dimensions when that route permits it. Render and audit the complete brief before treating whitespace as unused. Keep a last passing source and render.
2. Reserve complete name/date/caption, family-label, illustration and inset envelopes. Remove excess outer margins and oversized panels, then balance neighboring groups and let terminated branches release their space. Bring merger parents near their successor without changing ancestry or implying proportional chronology.
3. Shorten avoidable connector loops and excessive rank gaps. For institutional influences use [short influence routes](influence-routes.md); for genealogical groups preserve partnerships and sibling order with the measured cohort/baseline routes. Keep the full source and target envelopes, a visible approach/departure and enough room for the complete head. A helper's geometric minimum is not automatically the readable target.
4. Keep independent relations distinguishable. A shared stroke is acceptable only when the relation contract makes it a real shared junction; accidental unrelated trunks, ambiguous crossings and hidden heads reject the revision. Do not shorten a route through a label, image or another entity.
5. Rerender and run the browser audit with the exact revised source. Compare the new and previous poster at the same display width and inspect dense detail at the same relative scale. Check the shortest link, longest label, busiest merger, influence endpoints and every reclaimed pocket.

## Accept only safe reductions

Accept a tighter revision only when all required content remains visible, labels and art remain readable, text/line/head envelopes do not overlap unrelated content, arrow contrast and terminal direction pass, and each relation can still be traced from its named source to its target. Review `unrelated-shared-run` warnings even when the technical report otherwise passes. Actual painted geometry, not just solver costs or page dimensions, establishes a gain.

Restore the last passing spacing when a local reduction fails. Move the particular neighboring group or shorten another detour before enlarging the whole page. Stop when no concrete safe reduction remains; report inspected comparisons rather than claiming a global packing optimum. Remaining gaps may be necessary routing or grouping space.

Keep the reference-level knowledge-density gate separate. A denser census cannot compensate for a hidden head or text collision, and a readable compact diagram cannot establish an unmeasured reference-density pass. Use [evaluation and repair](evaluation.md) for the full visual acceptance contract.
