Everything the skill can do

Use D3 when the geometry, data binding, motion, interaction, or exported SVG itself is part of the product—not just a standard dashboard preset.

01

Quantitative charts

Bars, lines, areas, distributions, uncertainty, dense comparisons, matrices, tables, and small multiples.

02

Networks & flows

Force layouts, trees, Sankey and alluvial paths, dependency graphs, lifelines, pipelines, and routed systems.

03

Maps & spatial geometry

Projections, choropleths, Voronoi regions, contours, cartograms, geospatial symbols, and scientific fields.

04

Motion & interaction

Replayable reveals, transitions, simulation settles, brushing, zoom, focus/context, linked views, and playback.

05

AI & system explainers

Attention, token sampling, caches, model flow, capacity grids, execution traces, queues, and operational diagrams.

06

Composition & audit

Score visible SVGs, find collision and reading-path problems, and recompose source marks on purposeful armatures.

07

Logos & textures

Parametric marks, mathematical mechanisms, wordmarks, seals, motion-ready identity systems, and SVG textures.

08

Portable output

Offline HTML, accessible standalone SVG, deterministic settled frames, palette contracts, and validation reports.
