What maximum means here

A measured ceiling, not decorative density.

Mermaid has finite indexed palettes and semantic role surfaces, but most families do not have a finite node limit. The gallery therefore distinguishes terminal capacity from advanced representative syntax instead of claiming that an arbitrary node count is maximal.

  • Colorset 1 is the standard red-neutral palette.
  • Colorset 2 is the explicit extended full-color comparison.
  • Every source, static SVG, and animated SVG is downloadable and hash-verified.
