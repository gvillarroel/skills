## Ranking and fallback rule

- Treat child access as a mandatory gate. Exclude adult-only, no-minors, 18+-only, and 21+-only entries before ranking. Retain child-permitted options with guardian, age, height, content, or family-hour conditions only when those conditions remain visible.
- Normal restaurants and bakeries with no adult-only evidence may be retained through a documented venue-type inference; do not describe that inference as an official age-policy verification.
- Build an eligible candidate pool per category after removing duplicates, permanently closed places, adult-only venues, and entries without enough evidence to identify the place or event.
- Rank the eligible pool in this strict order: cultural priority, international or multicultural experience, affordability, then secondary quality. Secondary quality uses family fit, evidence, proximity, rating confidence, uniqueness, and timing where relevant.
- Treat affordability as entry cost rather than subjective value. Free-to-enter shopping districts do not imply that purchases are free.
- Publish up to 50 ranked entries when the eligible pool has at least 50 candidates.
- When the verified eligible pool has fewer than 50 candidates, publish the top half of that pool, rounded up. This is the operational interpretation of the user's 50% fallback instruction.
- Record the eligible pool size, published count, scoring inputs, and source URLs so the selection is auditable.
