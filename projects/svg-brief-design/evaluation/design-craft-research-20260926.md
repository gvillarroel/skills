# Contour design and print craft: development hypotheses

Research date: 2026-09-26. This is a new development proposal, not evidence of
improvement. Only public svt6 development outputs and primary design sources
were consulted. No private task content or outcomes enter these hypotheses.

## Sources and proposed transfer

- [Getty: Principles of Design](https://www.getty.edu/education/teachers/building_lessons/formal_analysis2.html)
  describes balance through visual weight, emphasis and directed movement.
  Transfer hypothesis: resolve the dominant gesture and broad mass/void shapes
  before decorating their interiors. Judge the visual center, not just a box.
- [The Met: Woodcut](https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/materials-and-techniques-printmaking-woodcut)
  explains that raised areas print and carved recesses remain blank.
  Transfer hypothesis: plan the white cuts as deliberate structural shapes.
  This does not justify tracing prints or prescribing thick lines for all old art.
- [The Met: Engraving](https://www.metmuseum.org/perspectives/materials-and-techniques-printmaking-engraving)
  describes controlled grooves, variable line width and spaced hatching for tone.
  Transfer hypothesis: select a coherent mark-making method and preserve gaps
  at delivery size; random roughness is not evidence of historic craft.
- [Butterick: Maxims of page layout](https://practicaltypography.com/maxims-of-page-layout.html)
  emphasizes hierarchy, consistent treatment, small visible adjustments and
  working outward from the text. Transfer hypothesis: compact labels should be
  sized from their actual content, with optical spacing after structural layout.
- [W3C SVG 2: cubic Bezier commands](https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/paths.html#PathDataCubicBezierCommands)
  defines control points and reflected handles. Transfer: use intentional tangent
  directions at joins; a mathematically smooth path still needs visual judgment.

These sources do not establish an SVG performance gain. The operational steps
below are our synthesis, tested as candidate interventions rather than treated
as museum-endorsed rules or universal numeric aesthetic targets.

## Why another attempt differs from earlier advice

The installed guide already mentions white space, weight, sparse notation and
smooth joins. The prior information candidate added required/supporting/optional
roles and reversible proof views; svt6 showed that its gains were concentrated
in HUD, while diagram and figure means barely changed. Repeating those general
principles would not be a new mechanism.

The public svt6 review records a mechanical body reduced to a slab with a narrow
channel, uniform modern diagram strokes with auxiliary ticks, and compact labels
that still expand into data panels. The proposals move the decision earlier:

| Candidate | New decision procedure | Observable intended effect | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| c: contour and counterform | Design a coarse pair of material and void shapes; define mouths/necks and contour transitions before interior detail; inspect the shape with details hidden | Broad legible openings and deliberate junctions; gesture survives without filler | Over-subtraction or lost identity; all brief requirements remain mandatory |
| d: print and optical finish | Choose relief, engraved or clean printed line grammar; control marks by their construction; fit actual content then correct optical spacing | Coherent mark character, restrained annotations and compact occupied form | Inappropriate antiquing; activate historic treatment only when requested |

No fixed element count, ink ratio, source silhouette, task identity, benchmark
score or reference path belongs in a candidate. All original geometry, renderer
and existing recipe APIs remain unchanged. The Trace interface patch is not
silently folded into either treatment. Pareto will retain complementary strengths
only if the full native qualification gates pass.
