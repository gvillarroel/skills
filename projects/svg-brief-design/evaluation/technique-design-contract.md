# Purpose-specific graphic technique

This development contract records the user's visual feedback of 2026-09-26.
It guides evaluator calibration and future skill experiments. It is not a claim
that a new skill has improved, nor a requirement to reproduce a purchased design.
The purchased SVGs remain outside the skill and generator workspace.

## What changed in the target

Technical validity and generic neatness do not establish professional artistic
execution. Judge whether construction serves the intended artifact: a printed
illustration, a compact identification strip, an integrated ornament or an open
mechanical insignia. Different outcomes can be equally good, but that conclusion
requires evidence that both resolve the intended technique.

| Purpose | Construction to reward | Failure to identify | Transferable generation operation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Robust vintage scientific illustration | A dominant explanatory trace, related axes, economical reference letters and coherent ink weight | A contemporary calibrated plot with decorative serif text; many unrequested guides; a weak hairline trace | Start with the trace and axes alone. Choose their weight together at intended size. Add only letters needed to explain the relationship. Give organic variation a coherent scale rather than random jitter. |
| Open space insignia | Black masses and bounded white openings jointly imply a connected body | A continuous black slab with incidental perforations; disconnected fragments; crowded joints | Establish a directional skeleton and a few masses. Design the white channels at the same time. Test whether the body remains legible across gaps before adding secondary hardware. |
| Integrated circular ornament | Repeated band-to-void transitions, controlled taper, legible continuation and intentional junctions | Several individually smooth curves with accidental tangencies, clotted crossings or irregular trapped slivers | Design one repeatable band/void relationship. Generate the repetition, then resolve each junction and the spaces between bands. Do not assume a parametric curve is finished composition. |
| Compact identification label | A small footprint with a few useful identifiers or navigation cues and a clear order of reading | A full equipment record with invented specification fields, multiple rules and excess area | Choose the shallow occupied shape and required information first. Allocate a dominant cue and brief supporting identifiers. Remove fields that do not serve the stated use. |

These rules are conditional. A measured contemporary plot may require ticks,
amplitude, period and an equilibrium guide. A specification record may require
voltage, serial number, lot and protection rating. Removing those requirements
is a failure. Thick lines, emptiness, simplicity and irregularity do not earn
points on their own.

## Evidence the evaluator needs

Describe a located relationship before calling it controlled or integrated:
which contour continues into which part, where an opening changes width, how
adjacent masses balance, where a crossing traps a sliver, or which annotation
adds a required explanation. Separate smoothness of an individual path from
quality of the complete arrangement.

Judge the small preview for principal structure and hierarchy; do not demand
thumbnail readability of every support label. Normalized renders establish
relative proportions and aspect ratio, not exact physical footprint. Raster
evidence cannot prove hidden path quality, mechanical reproducibility, printing
history or behavior at other export sizes. Validate those separately when the
task calls for them.

## Short briefs for future generation

These are quoted Spanish source examples. They add intended use without giving
exact positions, path geometry, element counts or reference artwork:

- Vintage: “Haz una gráfica de oscilación con aspecto de libro antiguo: trazo
  negro robusto, ejes claros y solo las pequeñas letras necesarias.”
- Insignia: “Crea una insignia espacial inclinada en la que las masas negras y
  los espacios blancos construyan juntos el cuerpo.”
- Ornament: “Quiero un marco circular de curvas enlazadas, con los encuentros
  y los espacios vacíos bien integrados y una sensación de movimiento fluido.”
- Label: “Diseña una etiqueta industrial pequeña, con un identificador y pocas
  señales útiles; debe ocupar poco espacio.”

## Evaluation and evolution boundaries

Keep origin and expected winner out of the judge input. Keep the curated SVGs
only in the trusted evaluator. Preserve all existing trials when changing the
rubric. Test reversed presentation, deliberately damaged examples and requests
whose purpose reverses the expected preference. A lower average generated score
does not by itself establish a better evaluator.

The existing outputs were generated under earlier briefs. Scoring them against
these clarified purposes is a retrospective development diagnostic. Only new
matched baseline and skill runs under the same clarified briefs can establish
generation improvement. Only a separate untouched evaluation can support a
promotion claim.
